DRAWING NOTES FOR THE THREE FLAGS
Kept beside the build so a later session does not "simplify" them again.

Each flag is drawn to its OFFICIAL proportions, not to a convenient box:
  UAE    1:2      viewBox 0 0 60 30
  Saudi  2:3      viewBox 0 0 60 40
  Qatar  11:28    viewBox 0 0 56 22   (the most elongated national flag)

UAE, Federal Law 2 of 1971
  Red vertical band at the hoist, one quarter of the length.
  Three equal horizontal bands over the remainder: green, white, black.

SAUDI ARABIA, 1973 specification
  Green field. The shahada in white across the upper middle, and a white
  sword beneath it with the blade pointing toward the hoist.
  The words are rendered as REAL ARABIC TEXT, not as decorative squiggles and
  not omitted. An earlier build reduced this flag to a plain green rectangle,
  which is not the flag. The script is set in whichever Arabic system face is
  present (Traditional Arabic, Arabic Typesetting, Noto Naskh Arabic) so
  nothing is downloaded and the browser does the shaping.
  Because the creed is written on it, this flag is shown WHOLE and undistorted
  wherever it appears. It is never cropped to a decorative sliver, never
  stretched, and never used as a faded texture behind body text.

QATAR, Emiri decision
  Maroon field, white band at the hoist, separated by NINE white points.
  Nine, because Qatar was the ninth member of the reconciled Emirates of the
  Gulf under the 1916 treaty. An earlier build drew the serration without
  fixing the count; it is now exactly nine.

WHY VECTOR AND NOT A PHOTOGRAPH
  The reference image is Getty Images, credited to Noushad Thekkayil via
  NurPhoto, so it is a licensed agency photograph rather than something to
  republish on a public page. Drawing the flags instead also means they stay
  sharp at any size, carry no external request, and their opacity is ours.
